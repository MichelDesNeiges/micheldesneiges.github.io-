About

Michel DesNeiges (also known professionally as Michel L. DesNeiges and Michel LeBlanc DesNeiges) is a lawyer, writer and longtime public-interest advocate based in Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada.

His work over the past three decades has crossed several fields, including environmental advocacy, law, public policy, teaching and community affairs. In the 1990s, he was active in the campaign to restore the Petitcodiac River and spearheaded the establishment of the Petitcodiac Riverkeeper. He later founded and directed the New Brunswick Environmental Law Society and went on to practice in criminal defence and other areas of public law.

He now maintains a limited and selective legal practice while devoting more time to writing and other projects and interests.

This personal website brings together biographical material, selected writings, historical records and media coverage relating to that work, along with other interests including travel, athletics and martial arts.

Biographical Overview

Selected aspects of Michel DesNeiges' public, professional and personal work to date.

Environmental Advocacy

In the 1990s, as a university student, Michel led Écoversité, an environmental advocacy group, and produced radio and television programs intended to raise public awareness of environmental issues.

He became particularly involved in the campaign to restore the Petitcodiac River. In 1999, he spearheaded the establishment of a Riverkeeper program for the Petitcodiac, an initiative that formed part of the broader public campaign that ultimately contributed to restoration of the river.

Legal Career

Michel began his legal career as director and lead counsel for the New Brunswick Environmental Law Society (2007–2014). In 2010, his private practice expanded from environmental law into criminal law, administrative law and child protection law. He practised mostly as a criminal defence lawyer between 2014 and 2024.

Teaching & Clinics

Beginning in 2007, Michel directed a public-interest environmental law clinic and lectured at universities in New Brunswick, sharing experience in environmental law, criminal law and advocacy.

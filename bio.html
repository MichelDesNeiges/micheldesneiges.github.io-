Professional Profile

Michel DesNeiges (also known professionally as Michel L. DesNeiges and Michel LeBlanc DesNeiges) is a lawyer and legal consultant based in Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada.

His legal career has included criminal defence, environmental law, administrative law, child protection law and other matters involving public law and public-interest advocacy. He now maintains a limited and selective legal practice, focusing on advisory work, targeted advocacy and matters of particular interest.

His broader background includes environmental advocacy, public policy, university teaching, writing and community affairs.

Professional Background

Selected aspects of Michel DesNeiges' legal, public-interest and professional work.

Legal Career

Michel began his legal career as director and lead counsel for the New Brunswick Environmental Law Society (2007–2014). In 2010, his private practice expanded from environmental law into criminal law, administrative law and child protection law. He practised mostly as a criminal defence lawyer between 2014 and 2024.

Environmental Advocacy

In the 1990s, Michel led Écoversité, an environmental advocacy group, and produced radio and television programs intended to raise public awareness of environmental issues.

He became particularly involved in the campaign to restore the Petitcodiac River. In 1999, he spearheaded the establishment of a Riverkeeper program for the Petitcodiac, an initiative connected to the broader campaign that ultimately contributed to restoration of the river.

Teaching & Clinics

Beginning in 2007, Michel directed a public-interest environmental law clinic and lectured at universities in New Brunswick on subjects including environmental law, criminal law and advocacy.
